## Brand & Style

This design system drives a modern, high-velocity B2B SaaS CRM experience. The aesthetic draws direct inspiration from product-led benchmarks like Linear and Stripe, balancing precision engineering with effortless legibility and commercial punch. 

The emotional signature is trustworthy, hyper-efficient, and engineered for high conversion. The visual language utilizes luminous backgrounds, micro-structural borders, precise typography, and data-dense UI previews that demonstrate instantaneous software speed. It establishes executive-level authority while remaining lightweight, approachable, and frictionless for sales leaders and growth operators.

## Colors

The palette establishes crisp contrast across light-mode marketing narratives and grounded enterprise authority:

- **Primary Brand Blue (`#1264F5`)**: High-saturation, decisive blue deployed across key conversion triggers, primary buttons, interactive state indicators, and active workflow tags.
- **Secondary Dark Navy (`#0B1830`)**: Grounding foundation for high-contrast CTA containers, the enterprise footer, and contrast accents.
- **Tertiary Metrics Emerald (`#10B981`)**: Dedicated conversion accent representing positive deltas, revenue gains, status validation, and pipeline health.
- **Hero & Canvas Surfaces**:
  - Hero Canvas: Soft atmospheric blue tint (`#EFF6FF`).
  - Secondary Section Shift: Slate-neutral alternate wash (`#F5F8FC`).
  - Card & Container Surface: Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Neutrals & Structural Lines**:
  - Dominant Heading/Body: Dark slate (`#0F172A`).
  - Supporting Muted Copy: Balanced slate gray (`#64748B`).
  - Structural Borders: Clean, low-contrast hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`).
  - Border Hover: Enhanced demarcation line (`#CBD5E1`).

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric crispness and modern warmth to balance enterprise structure with consumer-grade software delight.

- **Eyebrow**: Uppercase styling with expanded tracking (`0.06em`) paired with 600 weight, creating an authoritative lead-in badge or teaser line.
- **Hero & Headings**: Negative tracking (`-0.035em` to `-0.02em`) tightens headlines, preventing loose character spacing at large display scales and delivering a compact, modern product feel.
- **Body & Editorial Content**: Maintained with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) using `#0F172A` for primary reading and `#64748B` for secondary clarity.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: Rendered in 700 bold with tabular figures enabled for data dashboards, conversion stats, and ROI proof points.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured 12-column grid with a maximum content canvas of 1240px, centered horizontally.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and variable outer margins maintaining a 1240px container. Section vertical padding scales between 96px (`6rem`) and 120px to establish clear thematic shifts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px outer canvas margins. Multi-column cards shift into 2-column stacked modules.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins. Horizontal layouts compress to single-column vertical flows with full-width primary interactions.
- **Component Padding Rhythm**: Internal padding across cards and interactive blocks strictly follows an 8pt metric: 16px (`space-md`) for compact widgets, 24px (`space-lg`) for cards and accordion rows, and 40px (`space-xl`) for elevated dashboard containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered ambient shadows, deliberate hairline borders, and tinted light fields:

- **Flat / Base**: `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and zero shadow for secondary items and standard list items.
- **Interactive Card Shadow**: Multi-stop diffused shadow for floating modules and feature blocks:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06);`
  accompanied by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Dashboard Mockup Elevation**: Product interface frames use high-tier dimensional elevation to float above the canvas:
  `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 20px 48px -12px rgba(18, 100, 245, 0.12), 0 32px 64px -16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14);`
- **Primary CTA Glow**: Button hover states utilize an anchor glow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(18, 100, 245, 0.4);`
- **Dark Island (Footer / Final CTA)**: Elevated dark layer (`#0B1830`) punctuated with an inner edge highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`) and diffuse ambient blur.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp enterprise utility with smooth, contemporary product geometry. 

- **Cards, Panels & Containers**: Standardized on a generous 16px (`1rem` / `rounded-2xl` equivalent) corner radius, softening dense layouts.
- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges)**: Built with an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius for crisp, immediate touch targets.
- **Product Mockup Viewport**: 20px to 24px outer border radius, mirroring native desktop application framing.
- **Eyebrow Badges & Status Indicators**: Full pill capsules (`9999px`) to distinguish meta labels from structural UI blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ('Get Started Free')**: Solid `#1264F5` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `15px` semibold, 8px radius. Padding: 12px 24px. Subtle transition on hover with `#0C54D4` background and primary CTA glow shadow.
- **Secondary CTA ('Book a Demo')**: Transparent fill with 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, `#0F172A` text, 8px radius. Padding: 12px 24px. Hover state shifts surface to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#94A3B8`.
- **Dark Mode Button Variant**: Deployed in the footer/dark CTA: Solid `#1264F5` button or secondary ghost button featuring a 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` with white text.

### Eyebrow Badges
- Inline capsule tag (`rounded-full`) with 4px vertical, 12px horizontal padding.
- Surface: `#EFF6FF` with a 1px border in `rgba(18, 100, 245, 0.2)`. Text: `#1264F5` in `14px` bold/uppercase. Frequently paired with a leading glowing emerald metric dot.

### Dashboard Mockup Frame
- Outer wrapper featuring 20px radius, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, pure white canvas, and elevated ambient dashboard shadow.
- Top control bar: 44px height, faux window controls, integrated pipeline view switches, and subtle gray breadcrumbs.
- Content body: Kanban board and contact tables showing conversion stages with `#10B981` metric badges (`+34% MRR`, `Closed-Won`).

### Cards & Grid Modules
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 16px radius, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 24px internal padding.
- Hover interaction: Micro-elevation (Y: -2px) with border transition to `#CBD5E1`.

### Interactive Accordion FAQs
- Container: Border-bottom separated items using `#E2E8F0`.
- Closed State: 20px padding vertical, `#0F172A` semibold title, accompanied by a clean chevron icon rotating 180 degrees.
- Expanded State: Soft background shift to `#F8FAFC` with 16px corner radius, revealing `#64748B` body copy with relaxed line height.

### Compact Founder Trust Card
- Horizontal flex card containing an avatar with a 1px ring, verified green checkmark (`#10B981`), 2-line quote in 14px italic, and founder name/title in `#0F172A` and `#64748B`.
- Compact footprint positioned directly below primary hero CTA buttons to eliminate conversion friction.

### Form Inputs
- 44px total height, 8px radius, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 14px text.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#1264F5` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(18, 100, 245, 0.15)` focus ring.
