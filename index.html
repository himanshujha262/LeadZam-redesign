Clear answers about setting up, connecting tools, and growing with LeadZam.

LeadZam is a high-velocity CRM engineered specifically for marketing and sales teams running paid Meta and Google lead campaigns. It combines instant lead ingestion, automated WhatsApp/SMS response pipelines, context-preserving lead drawers, and closed-loop revenue reporting inside one clean workspace.
LeadZam connects natively to Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Lead Ads), Google Ads (Lead Extensions & Search forms), WhatsApp Business Cloud API, Gmail, Microsoft Outlook, and Google Sheets. We also offer incoming webhook endpoints for custom landing pages.
We leverage direct official OAuth webhooks from Meta Graph API and Google Ads API. When a prospect taps "Submit" on an ad form, the data is pushed to your LeadZam workspace in under 3 seconds without delayed polling cycles or brittle third-party connectors.
